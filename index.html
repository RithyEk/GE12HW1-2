Homework 1: Unit 6 Lesson 1 (Unreal Conditionals & Mixed Time Frames)

Exercise 1: 2nd vs. 3rd Conditionals

💡 Sample Guide: Choose the correct conditional structure matching past/present contexts.
1. If I _________ (take) the umbrella this morning, I _________ (not get) wet in the sudden rain right now.
2. If she _________ (have) more free time next weekend, she _________ (join) us on the trip.
3. If they _________ (leave) earlier yesterday, they _________ (catch) the train on time.
4. If he _________ (know) how to cook well, he _________ (prepare) dinner for us tonight.
5. If we _________ (study) harder last semester, we _________ (pass) all our final exams.
6. If you _________ (speak) English fluently, you _________ (get) that job last month.
7. If I _________ (be) you, I _________ (accept) the job offer immediately.

Exercise 2: 3rd vs. Mix1 vs. Mix2 Conditionals

💡 Sample Guide: Compare 3rd (Past-Past), Mix1 (Past condition -> Present result), and Mix2 (Present continuous condition -> Past result).
1. If she _________ (not spend) all her money on clothes every weekend, she _________ (buy) that laptop last month.
2. If they _________ (leave) for the airport earlier yesterday, they _________ (catch) their flight on time.
3. If it _________ (rain) heavily last night, the streets _________ (look) very wet today.
4. If he _________ (not drive) so recklessly every single day, he _________ (not get) into that car accident last week.
5. If we _________ (study) together in the library yesterday afternoon, we _________ (pass) the history test.
6. If my phone _________ (not break) yesterday, I _________ (not struggle) to contact my friends right now.
7. If Dara _________ (not play) video games all through the night, he _________ (finish) his project yesterday.

Exercise 3: Comprehensive Review (Mixed Types - 3 Options)

💡 Sample Guide: Choose the correct conditional form from 3 options.
1. If my phone _________ (not break) yesterday, I _________ (call) a rideshare right now.
2. If he _________ (take) the subway every day last week, he _________ (get) stuck in that major traffic jam.
3. If they _________ (not run) during PE class yesterday, they _________ (not feel) so tired right now.
4. If I _________ (go) to bed early last night, I _________ (not yawn) during class right now.
5. If she _________ (study) hard for the grammar test last week, she _________ (not worry) about her final score today.
6. If we _________ (study) together in the library yesterday afternoon, we _________ (prepare) for the big exam properly.
